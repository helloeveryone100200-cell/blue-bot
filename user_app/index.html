WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Help & Support

Quick answers to common questions about your Blue Bot dashboard.

How do I find a video?

Use the Public Library search to look up a video by its title or ID, then choose Watch in Telegram.

How do I save a video for later?

Choose Save video on a public video card. Your Saved Videos collection is private to your account and can hold up to 100 videos.

When does a referral count?

Blue Bot counts a referral when a new user starts the bot through your personal referral link. Opening your own link or an existing user's account does not create a new referral.

Why is my account restricted?

If your account is marked as restricted, some bot features may be unavailable. Contact the bot owner if you believe this is a mistake.

How is my dashboard information protected?

The dashboard verifies your Telegram Mini App session with the server and shows information for your own account. Saved video IDs are stored on your account record.
